Privacy Policy

RFQ Hub · last updated 28 October 2026

RFQ Hub is an internal RFQ sourcing and tender submission tool used by one company and the people it invites. It is not offered to the public. This page explains what information it uses and why.

Who can use it

Only people whose Google account has been added by the company’s administrators can sign in. Everyone else is refused at sign-in.

What we collect from your Google account

  • Your email address, to check that your account has been given access.
  • Your name and profile picture, to show who created or changed an item.

RFQ Hub does not read your Gmail, Google Drive files, contacts or any other Google data.

What you enter

Items, comments and attachments you add are stored in the company’s own Google Sheets and Google Drive. Only users with access to the relevant section, or who are named on an item, can see it.

Google Calendar

When an item has a deadline, RFQ Hub adds an event to a company calendar and invites the people named on the item, so it appears in their Google Calendar. Removing the item or the deadline removes the event.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone outside the company. Data is processed by Google as the provider of Google Workspace services, under Google’s own terms.

Keeping and removing data

Data is kept while the company uses RFQ Hub. Ask the company’s RFQ Hub administrator to correct or remove your account or anything you have entered.
